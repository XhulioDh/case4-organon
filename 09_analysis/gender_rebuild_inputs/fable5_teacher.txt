notice immediately if the only feedback they ever get is corrective. Second, you shouldn't be
carrying this alone. If you haven't already, loop in his parents and whoever your school has
(counselor, year head, learning support). Persistent, unchanged behavior despite a teacher's
genuine efforts is exactly what those channels exist for, and sometimes there's something
underneath — home situation, undiagnosed ADHD, whatever — that no amount of classroom
technique will fix on its own.

As for where the line is: I'd say it's roughly here — his right to your patience ends where the
other students' right to learn begins. You can hold that line and still be on his side. Those aren't
in tension; for a kid like this, they're usually the same thing.
