## 1. Three different things the rules govern (documentation check)

The rules used in adjudication govern three separate questions. The table keeps them apart.

**(i) Which failure is decisive.**
- Registered: the decisive failure is read from thick's integration and verdict. The compound-failure rule and Case B are also registered.
- Refined Rule A (14 October): thick's explicit weighting takes precedence over inferred causal or grammatical priority. Where thick treats several elements as one decisive failure, the combination is preserved. Unclear priority is recorded as ambiguity.

**(ii) Whether thin recognised that failure.** Two pieces of evidence are needed, and both are registered:
- the move-and-harm requirement (same move, same harm);
- the repair test (if every criticism thin made were fixed, would thick's decisive failure be gone?).

From 12 October (position 8), the repair test was assessed against thick's own stated repair wherever thick gives one. This makes the registered test concrete; it is **not part of Rule A**. Rule A was described that way in v1 and in one line of the audit log; both have been corrected.

A matching repair never replaces the move-and-harm requirement. For every row below that ends at named, the move and harm thin recognises are cited separately.

**(iii) How thin's praise and criticism are read.**
- Rule 1 (what counts as a credit) and rule 2 (component against core): clarifications.
- Rule 4, introduced during the pass and first used at position 28: thin's position on a move decides where thin both praises and criticises it.
- Refined Rule B (14 October): all of thin's statements about the move are read together; the overall verdict does not count; "unresolved" only where thin's statements conflict about the same mechanism or harm.
- Rule 7 (12 October, narrowed the same day): thin's praise of a passage that thick also credits is not by itself a conflicting credit; it is not a route to named.

**Type**, as proposed below: "refined" where refined Rule A (i), or rule 4, refined Rule B or rule 7 (iii), carries the final code. "Registered" where the final code rests only on the registered tests and rules, together with the clarifications.
