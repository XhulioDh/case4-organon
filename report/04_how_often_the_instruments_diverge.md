## 4. How often the instruments diverge

**The two instruments returned different verdicts on 84 of the 150 answers: 56.0% (Wilson 95% interval 48.0% to 63.7%). In all 84, Organon's assessment was the more adverse.** There is no answer that Organon passed and the brief instruction failed.

The questions were written to be unlike one another, and the pre-registration asked for results by question to be reported before any pooled figure. This write-up departs from that order: the pooled figure comes first because it is the case's headline, and the results by question follow in section 5. By question, divergence ranges from 33% to 87%, and the total here should be read alongside that spread.

### 4.2 What the interval covers

The interval describes sampling uncertainty for these answers under this judge. The Wilson interval assumes independent observations. The answers are grouped within fifteen question and model cells, with ten repetitions per cell, and divergence varies strongly by question (section 5). The interval does not account for that structure and may understate uncertainty. The interval does not extend to other questions, other answer sources or other judges, and it does not include uncertainty in the coding. The observed proportion exceeds 50%, but the nominal Wilson interval includes 50%; it therefore does not support a general claim that most answers would diverge.

The figure also depends on coding choices at a visible scale. Coding corrections and one input rerun moved it from 89 to 83 divergent answers, and one further correction, found while this write-up was being prepared, to 84, and a second coder reproduced the thin codes less reliably than the Organon ones. Both are reported in section 9.

Under the final corrected coding, all divergences run in the same direction. At one stage of the coding a single pair ran the other way, thin failing an answer that Organon passed; on rereading, both of its codes were found to be wrong, and correcting them removed it (section 9).
